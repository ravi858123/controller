BT Control turns your Android phone into a real Bluetooth HID gamepad. Pair
it like any other wireless controller with a PC, laptop, or console — no
companion app on the host, no root required.

Features:
- Analog joystick, D-pad, and 8 standard face/shoulder buttons
- Up to 8 custom, user-defined buttons you can add, rename, and remove
- Pairs with any already-bonded Bluetooth device
- No trackers, no ads, no non-free dependencies

Note: your phone's Bluetooth chipset must support the HID Device role. Most
modern Android devices (Android 9 / API 28 and up) do.
